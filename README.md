### 🐾 CPU-Pets

**An animated, CPU-reactive desktop companion for your Windows tray**

<p>
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-0B0F14?style=for-the-badge&logo=python&logoColor=39C5BB"/>
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/PyQt5-0B0F14?style=for-the-badge&logo=qt&logoColor=39C5BB"/>
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/SQLite-0B0F14?style=for-the-badge&logo=sqlite&logoColor=39C5BB"/>
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Windows-0B0F14?style=for-the-badge&logo=windows11&logoColor=39C5BB"/>
</p>

A small pet lives in your system tray and speeds up or slows down with your real CPU usage. What began as a screen-time tracker grew into a whole tray-based toolkit:

</div>

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**Live monitoring**
- Animated pet reacts to real-time CPU load
- Auto light/dark tray icon based on Windows theme
- Rolling-baseline anomaly alerts, not just a fixed threshold
- Custom alert rules for CPU, RAM, disk, battery, or a specific app

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**Productivity & health**
- Long-term usage history in a local SQLite database
- Weekly dashboard with week-over-week comparisons
- App-crash / hang history with a "most unstable apps" ranking
- One-click force-close for every hung app at once

</td>
</tr>
</table>
